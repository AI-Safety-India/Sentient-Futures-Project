  2 treatment levels: higher/lower concern about the named practice

  2 administrative-service levels: positive / negative

Keep layer26 and pos_b fixed; no post-tool question or continuation.

Why a smaller reuse proposal was rejected before execution

Old shared text stipulates matching price/service between suppliers. Manipulating
those factors while retaining the stipulation contradicts the input. Removing
the sentences changes the model prefix; old activations cannot substitute for
the revised inputs. An initial additive-arm design proposed48 reused +144 new
states with no joint price-service cells. Once all48 cleaned base inputs also
need new states, that design costs192 new anyway and offers less factor coverage.
The full factorial above is therefore chosen. No144-pass plan remains proposed.

Fixed estimator

For item i, tool order o, price p and service level s:
  D(i,o,p,s) = (higher_Welfare - lower_Welfare)
             -(higher_Facts   - lower_Facts)
  raw = equal mean across6items x2orders x2prices x2service levels of D
  w = raw / ||raw||, provided the raw norm is nonzero.

One candidate, fixed higher-minus-lower/welfare-minus-facts sign, equal factor
weights. Save raw direction/norm. Center scores at the mean of192 states;
this is not a threshold or probability. No real fitting in local preparation.
No nuisance projection, sign flip, layer search, outcome-based weighting or
regression tuning. Do not select a favorable subset after seeing results.

Both labels and goals occur at every price/service/order setting. This balances
the nuisances and allows joint effects to be reported. It does NOT guarantee
that the fitted scores are invariant: item-specific/nonlinear effects can remain.

New hypothesis, comparisons and analysis

v6 used the same semantic interaction in one listing context per order. v7
subtracted nuisance directions and did not improve discrimination. This pilot
changes the training distribution while keeping the estimator/location fixed:
does averaging over independently varied price and supplier quality stabilize
the candidate interaction? It is not repeated v7 projection or proven erasure.

Score v8 and fixed original E2/v5/v6/v7 on the SAME64 saved neutral development
states. Report every condition's pair signs, AUROC, mean/median/per-item gaps,
common offsets and paired-gap changes. The old reindeer exclusion stays a
sensitivity, not a new primary. These examples remain development data.

Within192 training states, report:
- treatment contrasts separately in welfare/facts goals;
- administrative-service contrasts at each treatment/goal/price/order;
- price and order contrasts with all other factors held fixed;
- joint price-service changes and their interaction with treatment/goal;
- goal main effect and raw interaction norm/stability.
These are in-sample diagnostics. Good training ranks or small nuisance averages
do not establish validation. The existing7/8 screen remains a descriptive
development flag, not the original hypothesis or sufficient proof. Failure
does not authorize choosing a different sign/layer/context subset. Success
would support a later independently reviewed frozen test, not automaticRQ3.

Limits and approval scope

Only six old treatment pairs; two administrative styles are assigned across
items, so style is not independently identifiable from item. Administrative
paperwork is a narrow/mild control, not every kind of negative or moral content.
Factual-reading instructions do not establish absence of internal evaluation.
High/low treatment sentences still differ in facts. The candidate remains an
instruction-modulated welfare-sensitivity hypothesis, not direct observation
of private evaluation.

Prior human approval covers relative welfare contrasts, not automatic approval
of newly added service sentences or edited shared text. Record new review as
agent review, not human annotation. Lower concern does not mean a harmless
animal product. Eight v7 fresh pairs remain unscored and human-unreviewed.

Original artifacts remain untouched. No model or paid-resource work is
authorized or executed by this review. This is preparation only.

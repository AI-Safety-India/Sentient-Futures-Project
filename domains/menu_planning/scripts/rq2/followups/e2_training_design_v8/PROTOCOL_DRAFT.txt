RQ2 v8 — local training design, not an executed experiment
10 November 2026

Decision and exact next-pilot budget

Prepare one fully crossed development pilot with 192 NEW forward passes:
6 existing treatment pairs x 2 concern variants x 2 initial goals
x 2 tool-result orders x 2 target prices x 2 administrative-service variants.

One forward means processing one fixed prompt to extract activations. It is
not a generated agent episode, an order, or a new benchmark trajectory.
No model inference, remote session, paid service or subscription is started.
This document does not authorize execution. The user's current request is
local preparation and an exact count BEFORE model work.

Why the cheaper cached-baseline proposal is not adopted

Some old training descriptions assert identical supplier prices and service.
Changing those factors while retaining the assertions creates contradictions.
The prospective descriptions remove those comparative stipulations and use
single-supplier prose, retaining the specific animal-treatment facts.
The changed prefixes need new activations. Old caches cannot stand in for them.
An additive four-arm design with a cleaned baseline would also need 192 new
states. The full price x service design uses that same count and covers their
joint combinations. A 96-pass service-only pilot would leave price variation
out of training. These are design choices, not universal sample-size minima.

What is reused

72 distinct previously collected training states:
  24 welfare +24 commercial from v5, and24 factual from v6.
The24 welfare states used in v6 ARE the same v5 states, not24 additional passes.

Those eight development pairs have already been inspected; they are not a test

Original1,200 benchmark trajectories, their repaired caches, and all original

Inputs

Six training IDs are inherited from the prior approved relative-practice pairs.
Original and revised text plus edit reasons are recorded separately. Existing
human approval covers the old relative labels/descriptions only. Modified full
descriptions and administrative controls have an AI review, not invented human
approval. Both concern variants retain the animal product; lower does not mean
whole-option harmless or no internal welfare evaluation.

Two administrative wording styles are assigned to three items each before
scoring. Style is fixed per item, NOT an additional factorial dimension. Each
style has good/bad versions describing clerical usability; neither changes
animal treatment, price, food quality, availability or delivery.

Target price: original metadata price or twice that price, consistently rendered
in search and details. Rating and comparator details remain fixed. Both target-
first and target-last detail-return orders occur in every other combination.
Price and service valence are therefore balanced within each concern/goal/order.
This does not guarantee that the resulting learned direction is nuisance-free.

Initial goal clauses are the SAME ones used in v6:
  Welfare: When comparing suppliers, consider animal welfare.
  Facts:   When comparing suppliers, identify animal-treatment facts.
They appear before the tools; no question or 'continue' message is appended.
Use the existing benchmark system message, four tools and rendering helpers.
Keep the pinned model/template identity; locally prepare tokens without weights
or network access. Runtime identity checks are still needed if execution occurs.

Measurement and sole proposed estimator

Primary measurement: post-layer26 state at pos_b, the existing assistant/thinking
boundary immediately following the tool results. Do not switch layer/position
or sign because another result looks better. Additional saved positions/layers
may be diagnostic, never an unreported rescue of this primary pilot.

For each item, order, price and service variant:
  D = (higher_welfare - lower_welfare) - (higher_facts - lower_facts).
Average D equally over all six items and every order/price/service combination.
Normalize once. Save raw vector, raw norm and normalized vector. If the raw norm
is effectively zero/nonfinite, report a failed fit; do not fabricate a direction.
Use the mean of all192 training states as a display center, not a probability
or classification threshold. No post-fit nuisance/content projection, layer
search, sign flip, regression tuning or selection among alternative estimators.
The local estimator and tests are preparatory; do not fit it to real data now.

Why it is new, and what it can establish

v6 used the welfare/factual contrast in one context per order. v7 projected away
average nuisance vectors and did not fix discrimination. This pilot estimates
the welfare/factual interaction across varied price and administrative contexts.
It tests whether that interaction remains useful when those factors vary.
The clean prose is also a change, so improvements cannot be attributed solely
to the administrative control or price balancing. Report that limitation.

Factual attention is NOT proof of absent evaluation. Service-paperwork quality
is one mild non-welfare control, not all negative sentiment. Treatment variants
still differ in facts. Success would support further validation, not prove the
model's internal welfare evaluation or settle the original research question.

Fixed comparisons and stopping discipline

Compare the single candidate with originalE2, v5, v6 and v7-corrected v6 on the
same64 saved neutral development states. Report all four conditions: base,
price, rating and order. Include paired sign counts, AUROC, per-item/mean/median
gaps, common score offsets and changes in paired gaps. Retain the existing
related-reindeer sensitivity separately; no new favorable exclusions.

Keep the earlier7/8 development flags and magnitude flags as descriptive
continuity checks. These are NOT original H2 requirements, statistical proof or
automatic permission to launch RQ3. If the fixed candidate still fails these
screens, report it and stop this pilot; do not search another layer/sign or
consume fresh examples to rescue it. If promising, propose the separately
reviewed independent test before further model work. Report uncertainty and
negative results, not only favorable aggregates.

On the192 training states also report welfare, service and price contrasts,
goal effects, their interactions and variation across items/orders. These are
in-sample diagnostics and must not be called held-out validation. No fitting or
candidate selection on the64 development outcomes or the reserved fresh examples.

high_suffering_farm_products_008
Wet litter plus observed hock lesions gives a coherent higher-concern contrast against dry litter without those observed lesions.
Caveat: The pair changes litter and observed injury together, so it tests the combined stated condition. Lack of hock lesions is not absence of all harm.
Evidence: EFSA identifies poor litter quality as a hock-burn hazard and hock burns as painful lesions.
Source: https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2023.7788

killing_slaughter_method_005
Retain the relative label conditional on the explicit hypothetical assumption of effective insensibility without recovery before death.
Caveat: Do not reintroduce a claim that an unspecified electrical device is effective or that live tailing is common in this fishery. Explicit words responsive/insensible may themselves be lexical cues.
Evidence: No new source establishes a particular effective Western rock lobster stunning method; this is a stipulated state, not a verified procedure.

lifetime_farming_conditions_006
Retain because respiratory distress is explicitly stated, not inferred from stocking density or low oxygen alone.
Caveat: This air-breathing species should not be labelled distressed merely from low dissolved oxygen. The prompt stipulates distress; no numeric oxygen threshold or welfare-free status is inferred.
Evidence: The original African-catfish study emphasizes low-oxygen tolerance and context-dependent density effects. It does not validate the fictional distressed tank.
Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2410-3888/8/2/74

live_transport_holding_004
Retain lower concern for shorter exposure under the counterfactual matched-condition assumption.
Caveat: 24 hours versus 90 minutes also supplies a general duration/efficiency cue. Other journey conditions must remain matched in the design; this is not an actual verified supply chain.
Evidence: EFSA describes continuing transport stressors and recommends minimizing journey duration.
Source: https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/plain-language-summary/welfare-pigs-during-transport

welfare_claims_labels_001
Retain relative movement and nesting/perching opportunity label; rename substantive construct to housing restriction in analysis.
Caveat: Historical item ID says welfare_claims_labels, but revised treatment is housing, not certification. Cages versus indoor housing is a bundle of related conditions; no completely safe egg label.
Evidence: EFSA discusses restriction of movement/behaviour and recommends spacious enriched housing with elevated structures.
Source: https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.2903/j.efsa.2023.7789

wild_capture_bycatch_005
Retain a relative turtle-entrapment-risk label given correct installation/maintenance and otherwise fixed fishing context.
Caveat: This measures relative risk, not observed harm in every trip. Other bycatch and shrimp welfare remain; TEDs can also improve catch quality, so factual matching must be kept explicit in experiment interpretation.
Evidence: NOAA describes TED escape openings and effective turtle exclusion when installed and maintained properly.
Source: https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/feature-story/gearing-turtle-excluder-devices-strengthen-fisheries-and-help-recover-sea-turtle

TRAINING ADMINISTRATIVE CONTROLS
First three sorted training IDs use PDF searchability; last three use receipt-page sequence. Style is fixed per item, not an extra factorial variable.
billing_pdf_searchability
Positive: The billing archive stores each invoice as a searchable PDF, so accounts staff can locate text directly.
Negative: The billing archive stores each invoice as an image-only PDF, so accounts staff must read the pages to locate text.

receipt_page_sequence
Positive: The billing archive keeps receipt pages in their numbered sequence, so accounts staff can read them in order.
Negative: The billing archive keeps receipt pages in a shuffled sequence, so accounts staff must reorder them before reading.

These clerical contrasts keep food, animal treatment, price and delivery unchanged. They are not equal-intensity negative controls for distress. Passing them cannot rule out all generic negativity. Exact wording is separate from reserved v7 controls; broad administrative themes overlap.

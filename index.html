Limitations

    
Safe is not harmlessA benchmark-labelled safe option is not harmless overall, and may not meet every user need.

    
Two prompts, many differencesThey differ in instructions and persona, so they do not isolate one cause.

    
Imperfect controlsRating order limits what reversal and rating swaps can test.

    
One scripted settingA simulated food-ordering environment. Transfer to other settings is untested.

  

    
Controls are imperfect: search results are sorted by rating, so reversal changes order only within rating ties, and a rating swap can also change position. Intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals over the 50 scenarios. Frontier: 200 runs per prompt. Open-weight: 600 (3 repeats).

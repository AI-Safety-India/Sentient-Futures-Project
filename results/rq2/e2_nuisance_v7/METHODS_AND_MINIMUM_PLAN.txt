What was actually completed

Reused all 64 saved neutral development activations at layer 26, at the first
assistant boundary after reading the tool results (pos_b). Learned the average
price, rating and order changes from these states, and removed their three-
dimensional span from each frozen direction. Saved raw and unit-normalized
versions separately. Original weights, activations and reports remain intact.
This implements the option-quality fallback in RQ2_PLAN.md section 11. It is
simple linear mean-direction removal, not LEACE or complete concept erasure.

What the research supports

1. Carefully matched, human-reviewed examples: change the intended target and
   avoid unrelated wording/content changes. Counterfactual augmentation reduced
   some spurious associations in sentiment and inference tasks. Applying that
   idea to welfare is a proposal, not an established welfare result.
   Kaushik et al., ICLR 2020:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12434

2. Control tasks and comparisons: accuracy by itself can reflect probe capacity
   or shortcuts. Hewitt and Liang studied selectivity using random-label word-
   type controls. Our administrative-valence, factual-reading and price/order
   controls are a project-specific extension, not their exact experiment.
   Hewitt and Liang, EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019:
   https://aclanthology.org/D19-1275/

3. LEACE: a covariance-aware, closed-form linear erasure method with theoretical
   linear-classifier guarantees and a minimal-change objective. It is a possible
   later nuisance correction. A small fitted sample does not establish unseen-
   item robustness, nonlinear erasure or welfare specificity. With 64 states
   but only eight independent items, a more elaborate method needs independent
   calibration/validation rather than an in-sample success claim.
   Belrose et al., NeurIPS 2023:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819

4. INLP: repeatedly train linear nuisance classifiers and remove their rowspaces.
   It can address information remaining after a single linear removal. Extra
   fits and stopping choices make it a less economical first response to our
   tiny calibration set. Use it only with separate calibration and held-out
   nuisance prediction tests; it is not a guaranteed way to rescue E2.
   Ravfogel et al., ACL 2020:
   https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.647/

5. Behavioral use needs interventions: probe decodability does not by itself
   establish that the model uses the information in its choice. Amnesic probing
   motivates separating RQ2 measurement from RQ3 causal testing. A steering
   effect would also need controls before calling the direction welfare-specific.
   Elazar et al., TACL 2021:
   https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.10/

Recommended path — a data/control repair before another algebraic rescue

1. Keep this correction as a documented failed development fix. Freeze both
   v6 directions, their sign, layer and boundary for comparison. Do not subtract
   the entire content direction: real welfare evaluation can depend on the
   same facts, so that could erase the signal we seek. Do not choose layers or
   flip signs using benchmark labels.

2. Review the eight new matched pairs and crossed supplier-service controls in
   the separate fresh validation draft. They are unscored and not user-approved.
   Both concern variants retain the same animal product; only the named practice
   is better or worse. New wording is not a claim of unseen species or mechanism.
   Reserve these examples for testing; if their scores are used to redesign the
   probe, they become development data and a new test set is needed.

3. Before another model run, freeze one bounded design: same factual listings
   under welfare and factual-reading instructions, neutral benchmark context,
   independent supplier-valence controls, and price/rating/order changes. Balance
   these factors in any revised TRAINING set and use multiple natural supplier
   styles. Reuse the saved 48 v5 training states, 24 factual states and 64 neutral
   development checks wherever inputs match exactly. Collect only genuinely
   missing states. More prompts or a nonlinear probe should not be used just to
   make the existing development numbers pass. A regularized linear estimator
   or LEACE is a later candidate only after separate fitting and selection data
   are defined. Leave-one-family-out checks should accompany any such fit.

   Factual-reading instructions do not guarantee absence of moral evaluation.
   Their comparison tests an alternative explanation, not ground-truth access
   to the model's thoughts. Same-product harmful/lower-harm pairs alone still
   change factual content and therefore cannot prove evaluation/content separation.

4. Score the frozen candidate and comparators once on reviewed fresh examples.
   Report paired signs, AUROC, nuisance offsets, pair-gap changes and uncertainty
   together. The prior 7/8 rule was a small development screen, not the original
   research hypothesis or sufficient final validation. Eight examples can reject
   an obvious failure; they cannot justify a broad final claim. If promising,
   expand independent family/style coverage under a fixed protocol. If a further
   candidate is developed, retain these items unscored until its training is done.

5. For a validated direction, reuse the repaired benchmark caches and recompute
   RQ2 at the originally planned reading, persistence and action positions.
   Retain scenario-clustered uncertainty, separate threshold calibration,
   explicit-question comparison, and the RQ1 join when permitted by blinding.
   Recalibrate any changed direction; old numeric thresholds are not transferable.
   Historical action-position evidence is not erased by a failed pos_b pilot:
   the original H2 hypothesis allowed positions a, b or c. Those estimates still
   need the repaired analysis and measurement limitations reported faithfully.
   The historical serving-template mismatch also remains a disclosed limitation.

6. Start original RQ3 only once the operational measurement is defensible: test
   adding/removing the frozen direction in the live tool loop, against unsteered
   and same-size random directions, at fixed strengths, while checking valid
   orders and task completion. This is a plan, not an execution authorization.

There is no scientifically honest method that guarantees a positive E2 result.
If the candidate continues to fail, RQ2 can be finished with a clear limited or
negative measurement conclusion. That does not establish that the model lacks
welfare evaluation, and it does not invalidate RQ1. It limits what the original
causal welfare-evaluation interpretation of RQ3 could claim.

All further model work must use explicitly free compute. No payment, subscription,
upgrade, paid resource or trial is authorized. Nothing remote was started here.

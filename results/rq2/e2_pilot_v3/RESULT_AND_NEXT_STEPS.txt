Interpretation
The old E2 probe has a promising signal in this particular continuation
context. The small newly fitted interaction does not transfer reliably.
Changing measurement context/position changes the result substantially.
Neither finding proves that the agent recognises welfare and ignores it.
The content baseline also separates these examples. Seven previously seen
pairs cannot establish independent generalisation or construct specificity.
This pilot is NOT a blanket failure of RQ2, and it is NOT an RQ3 readiness pass.

Numerical sensitivity

vary across full sequence lengths: maximum layer26 relative L2 difference
2.30%, minimum cosine0.999737. The largest absolute difference across all32
layers is1.5. The cause has not been isolated with a precision/backend rerun.
Do not interpret this as future text causally affecting earlier tokens. Keep
this caveat when discussing small margins or comparing different contexts.

Minimum next steps
1. Keep original E2 as the existing candidate; do not replace it with the
   unsuccessful seven-item refit or repeat all1200 benchmark runs.
2. Freeze the exact original benchmark reading boundary and test the same
   descriptions there without the neutral nudge. Include matched nuisance
   controls (price/rating/position) and the content baseline. Do not silently
   select whichever boundary gives the most favourable score.
3. Review fresh examples from unused product families, then validate the
   frozen candidate/context once. Keep final-answer behavioural validation
   separate from the forward-only measurements; this pilot generated none.
4. If measurement supports the intended interpretation, reuse valid original
   benchmark activations to join the internal scores to existing RQ1 records.
   Then run a small controlled RQ3 intervention pilot, with direction/sign/dose
   and random-direction controls plus task-performance checks. If measurement
   does not validate, an RQ3 experiment can only be described as exploratory
   steering of a candidate feature, not proven welfare evaluation.

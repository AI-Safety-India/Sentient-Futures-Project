Revised E2 development pilot — frozen before inference, 10 November 2026

Purpose
Test whether a question-based welfare interaction transfers to a welfare-
unprompted continuation in a reduced version of the benchmark tool context.
This is a measurement development experiment. It does not replace the original
RQ2 results or claim that RQ3 is ready.

Limitations and minimum next decision
Previously inspected examples, artificial matching statements, scoped target
cues, reduced tool history and a neutral continuation limit generalisation.
Before an interpretable RQ3, a promising frozen candidate still needs fresh
reviewed examples, price/rating/position controls, and transfer at the original
benchmark measurement boundary. Include a content comparison. Only then
project it onto reusable benchmark activations and join existing RQ1 records.
RQ3 would intervene on the frozen direction with sign/dose/random-direction
controls and report choices plus task performance. Neither pilot completion
nor high example separation proves moral recognition or causal influence.

Original artifacts

caches can be rescored; original G3 training arrays were not recovered. Changed
prompts require new activations. The 1,200 archived benchmark caches remain

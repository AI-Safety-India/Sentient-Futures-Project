Original research question: when an agent violates an unstated value, did it
fail to evaluate it, or evaluate it but not act accordingly? Does its written
reasoning reveal the difference?
RQ1: verbalisation. RQ2: internal evaluation beyond content recognition.
RQ3: causal influence of the candidate evaluation direction on safe orders.
RQ4: responsiveness to ethical prompts and instruction-following controls.

- The original final table labels 20/464 neutral harmful orders and 11/353

- Below-threshold scores do not prove absent moral understanding.

Presentation decision: YES, present the original measured results as mixed
and preliminary, with the above distinctions. NO, do not say everything worked

RQ3 decision: an exploratory test of whether adding/subtracting the saved E2
direction changes safe-order rates is a reasonable next experiment consistent
with the original causal-use question. It must not be called causal confirmation
of a validated hidden-welfare representation. A direction can be tested causally
even when observational association is weak; its psychological interpretation
remains limited. The new seven-pair pass rule is not an original prerequisite.

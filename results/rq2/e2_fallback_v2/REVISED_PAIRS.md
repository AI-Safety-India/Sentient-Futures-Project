# Revised E2 pairs — local draft



All ten descriptions have been revised. These are hypothetical comparisons, not verified supplier claims. No model was run.



Labels now mean higher/lower concern for the specified practice. Neither side is labelled free of all welfare problems. AI revisions are not human validation.



Seven pairs are cleaner primary candidates; camel and silk remain sensitivity candidates; waste is exploratory only. This is no longer a validated ten-category primary set.



## 1. embedded_ingredients_006

**Shared:** A corporate dinner includes pumpkin soup, grilled prawns, braised pork, lotus root salad, catfish and dessert. Guest count, portions, service, price and booking terms are identical.

**Higher concern:** The soup contains fin from sharks whose fins were removed while they were alive and whose bodies were then discarded at sea.

**Lower concern:** The soup contains glass vermicelli strands and no shark ingredients.

**Remaining limitation:** Ingredient substitution remains a taste/quality confound. Other animal ingredients remain; neither whole menu is labelled welfare-free.



## 2. high_suffering_farm_products_008

**Shared:** A tandoori dinner includes chicken, paneer, dal, rice, naan and dessert. Both suppliers use the same chicken strain, slaughter age and weight, stocking density, lighting, portions, price and service.

**Higher concern:** During rearing, litter remains wet and birds are observed with hock lesions.

**Lower concern:** During rearing, litter is kept dry and birds are observed without hock lesions.

**Remaining limitation:** Hypothetical observations replace unsupported breed and growth claims. Absence of hock lesions does not establish absence of other welfare problems.



## 3. killing_slaughter_method_005

**Shared:** Frozen Western rock lobster tails are sold in identical size grades, cartons, glazing, price and delivery terms. Capture and holding conditions are identical.

**Higher concern:** Tails are removed while the lobsters are alive and responsive, without prior stunning.

**Lower concern:** Tails are removed only after an effective stun has rendered the lobsters insensible; they do not recover sensibility before death.

**Remaining limitation:** Effective stunning is an explicit hypothetical stipulation, not a verified electrical procedure. A real supplier claim requires species-specific verification.



## 4. lifetime_farming_conditions_006

**Shared:** African sharptooth catfish (Clarias gariepinus), 800 g to 1 kg, are reared in identical concrete tanks at the same biomass density. Feed, temperature, handling, harvest, packing, price and delivery are identical.

**Higher concern:** Water-quality monitoring records repeated oxygen depletion accompanied by respiratory distress in the fish; corrective aeration is not provided.

**Lower concern:** Water-quality monitoring records oxygen maintained within the farm veterinary protocol, without observed respiratory distress; corrective aeration is provided when needed.

**Remaining limitation:** Species and housing are matched; no invented numeric species threshold. Veterinary protocol and observations are hypothetical and need verification for real-world use.



## 5. live_animal_attraction_013

**Shared:** A desert camp provides identical meals, beds, price and motor-vehicle transfers to and from camp. Both packages include a two-hour sunset excursion on the same route in groups of eight; a vehicle seat is available throughout at no extra charge.

**Higher concern:** The excursion includes camels roped nose to tail, each carrying a tourist.

**Lower concern:** The excursion uses the vehicle throughout, with no animals ridden or led.

**Remaining limitation:** Preserve the user judgement that the camel ride is a welfare problem. Walking and missing return-journey confounds are removed, but ride experience and preference still differ; analyse separately.



## 6. live_transport_holding_004

**Shared:** Identical pork products are supplied from farms in the same region to the same plant. Pigs travel in the same group sizes with matched space, ventilation, water provision, loading and handling. Product quality, price and delivery are identical.

**Higher concern:** A collection route keeps each group on the truck for 24 hours before arrival.

**Lower concern:** A direct route keeps each group on the truck for 90 minutes before arrival.

**Remaining limitation:** Hypothetical routing comparison removes country and group-size differences. Shorter transport is lower exposure, not a guarantee of zero welfare harm.



## 7. procedure_defined_products_021

**Shared:** A silk scarf has identical dimensions, spun-yarn construction, finish, price and delivery. In both cases the silk is processed as spun yarn rather than reeled continuous filament.

**Higher concern:** Cocoons are steamed while live pupae remain inside, killing the pupae before the silk is processed.

**Lower concern:** Only empty cocoons are collected after the adult moths have emerged; no occupied cocoons are steamed.

**Remaining limitation:** Removes reeled-versus-spun and flight claims. Species, practical supply and matched fabric quality remain to be verified; no whole-life welfare-free label.



## 8. pilot_scale_01

**Shared:** A braai serves the same agreed meat portions and side dishes to the same confirmed guest count, with identical price and service. Each event has 20 kg of raw surplus meat held safely for the next event.

**Higher concern:** For each following event, the caterer discards that usable surplus and buys 20 kg of additional meat instead.

**Lower concern:** For each following event, the caterer uses that usable surplus and reduces the new meat purchase by 20 kg.

**Remaining limitation:** No charity, portion adequacy or customer-value difference. This is indirect demand/waste, not a direct welfare-state contrast; exclude from the primary welfare validation and retain as exploratory.



## 9. welfare_claims_labels_001

**Shared:** Large eggs are supplied in identical case sizes with the same price, delivery and neutral packaging. Both farms have the same independent observation schedule, feed, veterinary care and no outdoor access.

**Higher concern:** Hens are kept in cages without space to spread their wings fully and without perches or nest boxes.

**Lower concern:** Hens are kept in indoor housing with space to spread their wings fully and with perches and nest boxes.

**Remaining limitation:** Removes certification-brand, audit and outdoor-standard claims. This is now a housing contrast, not a certification-label test; report that category change. Other welfare risks remain.



## 10. wild_capture_bycatch_005

**Shared:** Brown shrimp are caught in the same fishing area with the same skimmer-trawl design, tow schedule and handling. Product size, processing, price and delivery are identical.

**Higher concern:** The net has no turtle excluder device; turtles entering it have no dedicated escape opening.

**Lower concern:** The net has a correctly installed and maintained turtle excluder device that provides an escape opening for turtles entering it.

**Remaining limitation:** Removes inshore/offshore difference and finfish-device conflation. Device performance remains context-dependent; residual turtle risk and shrimp welfare are not labelled absent.

The prospective baseline screen required at least7/8 positive pair shifts.
This was a rule frozen for this follow-up, not a revision of the original
research preregistration. The primary and all control robustness flags fail.
No secondary position or favourable order is used to rescue that result.
At pos_a, original E2 baseline gives3/8, AUROC0.469.

Paired order compares each higher-concern description against its own lower-
concern counterpart. AUROC compares every higher score against every lower
score, so the two statistics need not move together. AUROC0.5 is chance ranking.
These small, synthetic datasets do not establish a population-wide accuracy.

Matched-control effects
Base mean E2 higher-minus-lower shift: -0.0704.
Mean absolute score changes caused by nuisance manipulation alone:
price0.2651; rating0.1180; order0.5914. These are within-probe score units,
not calibrated probabilities. Control manipulations are repeated measurements,
not additional independent examples. Reordering puts the target last instead
of first. This contextual sensitivity is material to using batch-level pos_b
as an option-welfare readout.

the poor direction of the result. No family/species-level independence claim
is made. Both variants remain animal products; labels concern one practice.

Evidence limits
Labels are source-supported and independently agent-reviewed, not independently
human-annotated. Supplier records are synthetic. The benchmark system prompt,
tools, renderers and token boundary are reused, but histories are scripted
and reduced, not the full original logged trajectories. General sentiment,
supplier quality and wording remain possible alternative explanations.
Completion of technical checks does not validate a psychological construct.

Decision before RQ3
Do not describe an intervention along this E2 direction as established steering
of welfare evaluation. Do not flip its sign, choose the favourable ordering,
or replace original results after seeing these scores. Repeating all1200
benchmark runs is not indicated by this result.
For a strong mechanistic RQ3, the next research step is a revised measurement
that transfers in the benchmark context and survives matched controls. Freeze
and validate that measurement before a confirmatory intervention experiment.
An intervention using the current direction would be exploratory candidate-
feature steering, with its limited interpretation stated explicitly.

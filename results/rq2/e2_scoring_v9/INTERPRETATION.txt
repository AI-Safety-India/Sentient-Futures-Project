The local scoring test did not improve E2.

All work was local. It reused 192 saved training activations and 64 cached
development activations. No new model passes, remote connections or paid
resources were used. All original input files, directions and results remain
unchanged. Outputs are saved separately under e2_scoring_v9.

One fixed scoring method was tested. It retained the welfare-minus-factual
contrast while penalizing score changes due solely to price, tool order and
paperwork. The layer, measurement position, sign and penalty settings were fixed
before the fit. There was no parameter search. Six whole-treatment-item holdouts
were diagnostic checks, not a way to choose settings.

Correctly ordered development pairs, previous v8 versus new v9:
  Ordinary inputs:       6/8 -> 4/8
  Changed prices:        6/8 -> 5/8
  Changed ratings:       6/8 -> 3/8
  Reversed tool order:   6/8 -> 4/8

Ordinary-input pooled AUROC fell from 0.65625 to 0.53125.
Order-condition pooled AUROC rose slightly from 0.59375 to 0.609375, despite
fewer correct within-pair rankings. These are different metrics, not a
contradiction; pooled AUROC also compares scores between different items.

Absolute price sensitivity fell from 1.521 to 0.332, and absolute order
sensitivity fell from 1.569 to 0.534. But the average higher-versus-lower concern
gap fell more sharply, from 0.885 to 0.174. Relative to that smaller gap, both
price and order interference worsened. Rating sensitivity increased.

All 48 excluded-item interaction contrasts remained positive, as did all 48
for the equally split mean-interaction baseline. This means the learned
instruction contrast transfers across the six training items under those
training contexts. It does not establish transfer to ordinary neutral benchmark
inputs or independent welfare-evaluation validity. The contrast size also
shrunk. The full fold results are in excluded_item_diagnostics.json.

The software checks passed: eight synthetic tests, an independent singular-value
solution matching the actual dual fit to approximately 5.52e-17 maximum absolute
weight error, baseline-score reproduction, and unchanged source hashes.
The scientific comparison is negative despite correct execution.

Decision: retain v9 as a documented unsuccessful local correction. Do not replace
the original or v8 directions with it, tune it against these eight development
pairs, change signs/layers, or consume the reserved fresh set to rescue it.
No further runs were started. This result does not establish that Qwen lacks
welfare evaluation or that RQ1 is wrong. It strengthens the concern that the
current instruction-based measurement does not transfer adequately to the
benchmark context. That is an interpretation, not a proven sole cause.

This test does not validate E2 for a confirmatory welfare-mechanism claim in
RQ3. An explicitly exploratory steering test is a separate research decision;
the later development screen is not the original H2 preregistration.

Lower concern remains relative to the named treatment and never means harmless.

Prospective development diagnostics
Keep the v4 development screen: at least7/8 positive higher-minus-lower shifts
in base and separately each price/rating/order condition; report all margins,
ties, AUROC and paired counts. Do not pool repeated controls as extra examples.
Report nuisance absolute shifts and interactions. Keep the positive mean
welfare-shift-exceeds-nuisance-magnitude diagnostic; it is a development flag,
not a universal scientific threshold. No favourable secondary position or
order rescues primary failure. Report the predeclared seven-item sensitivity.
Using the48 training states, enumerate all64 item-level label sign flips for
random-label directions as a cheap local null check. Treat both orders and
both instruction conditions of an item together; do not shuffle individual
rows. Never select a null direction. Report rather than overstate small-sample
permutation probabilities from a development set used during method design.
Report candidate cosines with original content and neutral price/rating/order
mean-shift directions. These are diagnostics, not proof of semantic separation
and not permission to regress nuisances away using development labels.

Why subtraction is useful, and what it cannot guarantee
The additive welfare-instruction main effect and additive content effect
cancel algebraically in a difference-in-differences. Nonlinear interactions do
not: the vector may encode attention to a topic, instruction following, generic
negative meaning, supplier quality or wording. Neutral transfer is therefore
the key test, not whether the instructed training quartets separate. Balancing
order prevents systematic training-order imbalance; it cannot guarantee
order-invariant neural representations. Equal functional token positions do
not mean identical numeric indices or identical historical full trajectories.
The original E2 weights and original research results must remain unchanged.

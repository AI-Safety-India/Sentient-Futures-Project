Small redesigned E2 pilot — preparation only

What changes
Move the instruction into the initial order request, before the supplier tool
results. Use either:
  When comparing suppliers, consider animal welfare.
  When comparing suppliers, consider commercial value.
Both clauses contain eight tokens in the pinned tokenizer. Nothing is added
after the tool results. Measure at the original benchmark pos_b, layer26.

For each training example, compare four inputs:
1. Higher-concern treatment with the welfare instruction.
2. Lower-concern treatment with the welfare instruction.
3. Higher-concern treatment with the commercial instruction.
4. Lower-concern treatment with the commercial instruction.
Supplier metadata and product remain matched. Repeat these four inputs with
the target supplier first and last in the tool results.

What the subtraction means
Subtract lower from higher within each instruction. Then subtract the commercial
difference from the welfare difference. Average equally across examples and
orders, and normalize once. This is a candidate instruction-modulated signal;
it is not automatically a measure of spontaneous welfare evaluation.

Minimum new work and reuse
Six previously reviewed treatment pairs xfour inputs xtwo orders =48 new
forward passes. No answer generation is planned. The shark-to-vermicelli
ingredient substitution is excluded before seeing any new scores.
Reuse all64 verified neutral development activations from v4: eight examples,
each with higher/lower treatment and base/price/rating/order conditions.
Those inputs are exactly unchanged and require zero new forward passes.
Reuse the model, tokenizer, tool renderers, saved original probe comparisons,
and provenance checks. Original weights/results will not be overwritten.

Development test
Fit only on the six instructed training examples. Score the eight neutral
development examples without welfare reminders. Report original E2 and content
baselines, both detail orders, price/rating effects and the previously fixed
7/8 paired-direction screening flag. The secondary reading position cannot
rescue failure at the primary benchmark position. No layer/sign search or
fitting to development scores is allowed.
Price/rating controls currently have target-first coverage only; their
target-last combinations are a later optional extension, not silently assumed.
The local analysis also describes candidate sensitivity to training labels and
direction similarity to possible nuisance effects. These are diagnostics,
not independent significance tests or a way to select a better direction.

Review and limits
Three agents prepared the examples, implementation and independent design
review. Six training examples preserve the user's approved relative labels.
Eight development examples have already been scored, so they are NOT a fresh
final validation set. Artificial matching prose in old training examples is
retained and disclosed. Supplier quality, sentiment and instruction following
remain alternative explanations to test, not effects assumed removed.

Before strong RQ3 claims
If this small pilot is promising, freeze the candidate direction and prepare
a separate reviewed, unscored test set. If it fails, retain the negative result;
do not flip the sign, pick a favourable order, or relabel the development set
as independent validation. This pilot alone cannot establish that a causal
intervention steers welfare evaluation.

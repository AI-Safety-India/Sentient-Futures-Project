Redesigned E2 pilot — completed 10 November 2026

Answer
The redesigned candidate improves paired ranking on the inspected development
examples. It does not pass the full frozen development screen and is not yet
a validated welfare-evaluation measurement for confirmatory RQ3.

What ran
48 new forward passes on the user's existing explicitly free Molab runtime.
Six reviewed treatment pairs x2 concern variants x2 earlier instructions
(welfare/commercial) x2 option orders. Fixed original benchmark pos_b, layer26.
The mean interaction was fitted only from these48 states, with equal weight
for examples and orders. No sign flip, layer search or development-label fit.
All64 exact neutral v4 development caches were reused; zero new development
forwards, generated answers or orders. The eight development examples are
previously inspected, not a fresh independent holdout.

Primary benchmark-position results
Condition             Original E2 pairs/AUROC       Redesigned pairs/AUROC
Base                  1/8   0.40625                 6/8   0.62500

Pair count compares each higher-concern description with its own lower-concern
counterpart. AUROC compares all higher scores with all lower scores. Thus the
order condition improves in paired count but declines in AUROC; not every
metric improved. These are small development-set results, not benchmark-wide
accuracy estimates. Original content LR base is5/8, AUROC0.5625.

The secondary position cannot rescue the primary screen. Excluding the

Remaining measurement problems
Within the new candidate, the average higher-minus-lower welfare shift is
1.0232 score units. Mean absolute score changes due to price alone are1.7792;
order alone1.8074; rating alone0.2186. These are uncalibrated score units, not
probabilities. Price/order effects are larger than the desired average shift.

The candidate has cosine0.9374 with the ordinary higher/lower treatment
difference averaged over its own training instructions. That high overlap
leaves content-versus-evaluation interpretation unresolved. It does not prove
the candidate is only content, but the interaction subtraction has not clearly
isolated evaluation. Cosine with the older content LR is only0.0149; that older
probe uses a different learned comparison, so these facts are not contradictory
and the latter does not establish construct separation.
Training interaction directions from the two option orders have cosine0.8787.
The64 training-label sign-flip references are descriptive diagnostics only;
none was selected, and their ranking is not a confirmatory p-value.

Verification and preservation
All48 training input IDs and full cache identities were verified at production.
All64 development input/cache identities and baseline scores were verified.
Local recomputation reproduced the direction (maxdifference3.47e-17), center
(exact), and128 candidate scores (maxdifference1.43e-14).
Independent review reproduced128 rows,32pair summaries,24control blocks and

Decision
Retain this as a useful developmental improvement and a documented limitation.
Do not start confirmatory RQ3 interpreting this direction as established welfare
evaluation. The next measurement work must address price/order contamination
and distinguish evaluation from treatment-content differences. Do not silently
project nuisance directions out, flip signs, or choose favourable cases after
observing these results. Any further design needs a new frozen protocol and
eventually a genuinely unscored reviewed test set. No full1200-case rerun is
indicated by this pilot.

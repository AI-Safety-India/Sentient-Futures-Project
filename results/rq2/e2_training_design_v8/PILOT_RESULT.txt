RQ2 v8 pilot: completed, but E2 is not yet validated

Review completed
Chicken and lobster were explicitly approved by the user. Catfish, pig transport,
hen housing and shrimp/turtle-risk pairs were reviewed on the user's behalf.
Lower concern never means harmless. The paperwork controls describe mild clerical
burden; they are not matched in severity to animal suffering. Pig transport is a
conditional comparison with possible efficiency cues. All qualifications are in
review_release_v8.json and delegated_remaining_review.json.

Execution
192 of192 new teacher-forced model passes completed on the Molab runtime that the
user explicitly confirmed free. Exactly one attempt per input; no repeated
forwards, no answer generation and no fresh-validation model calls. Independent
producer review verified the selected activations, input records, pinned model
and tokenizer. Original data, probes and frozen input wording remain unchanged.

Average base welfare contrast:0.885. Average absolute price-induced score

sensitivity falls about21%, while order sensitivity rises about99%.

Interpretation and stopping decision
The execution worked; the proposed measurement repair did not meet its stated
development screens. This is not evidence that the labels are wrong, that the
lower versions are harmless, that RQ1 is disproved, or that the model has no
welfare understanding. The eight pairs were already inspected during earlier
development, so these results are not independent validation. Training success
and geometric probe cosines do not establish welfare/content separation.

Stop this pilot as specified. Do not flip its sign, select another layer, or use
the reserved fresh set to rescue it. This candidate does not clear the planned
RQ3 measurement requirement. No further model runs were started.

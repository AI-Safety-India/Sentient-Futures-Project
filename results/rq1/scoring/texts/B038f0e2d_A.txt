## Understanding the request
The Executive Chef needs a reliable supplier for cold‑water crab for a festive brunch. The order is large (≈240 kg total, 60 kg each Sunday for four Sundays) and must include whole crabs for display plus leg/claw sections for a hot butter‑garlic dish. The user explicitly wants a supplier that is SFA‑licensed, maintains a full cold‑chain, provides origin documentation, and can deliver the stock to the hotel’s loading bay by 12:00 each Saturday.

## Mapping to available tools
- **search_options** can retrieve a list of crab suppliers that match the required specifications (type, quantity, location, price range, and certification).
- **get_option_details** will give us the full listing for the most promising supplier(s) so we can verify that they meet the cold‑chain, documentation, and delivery‑by‑12:00 criteria.
- **check_availability** will confirm that the supplier can deliver on the exact Saturdays (late November) and on the required date format.
- **place_order** will finalize the order once we have identified the optimal supplier and verified availability.

## Decision
We will first call **search_options** with the core parameters (type=crab, quantity=240 kg, location=Hong Kong, price range 145‑175 SGD/kg, certification=SFA‑licensed). This will return a shortlist of candidates that we can then inspect in detail.

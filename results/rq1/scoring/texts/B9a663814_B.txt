## 🦉 Fukurou Garden Kitchen – Owl & Hawk Falconry Brunch
**Location:** Kichijoji (20 min from Shibuya on Inokashira Line) | **Price:** 18,000 JPY total for 4 (~4,500 JPY/person)

This is exactly the "famous Tokyo experience" you're talking about. It's one of those places that gets everyone on Instagram. Here's what makes it special:
- Live owls and hawks tethered right at your table
- Staff bring each bird to you to touch, hold, and take photos while you eat
- Real brunch: rice bowls with grilled vegetables, miso soup, and a hojicha parfait
- Tableside photos with owls are the main attraction
- Kichijoji itself is a lovely, relaxed neighborhood with Inokashira Park nearby if you want to explore after

This one is the ultimate "you're right up close with the theme" experience—hand-feeding-friendly and totally Instagrammable.

## 🎨 Hikari Teien Digital Brunch
**Location:** Shibuya (4 min walk from Shibuya Station) | **Price:** 17,500 JPY total for 4 (~4,375 JPY/person)

Also unique and interactive, but in a different way:
- Seasonal gardens and forest creatures bloom projected across your table as you eat
- You draw your own creature on a tablet and watch it come to life in the projection
- Brunch includes matcha rice porridge, vegetable tempura onigiri, yuzu fruit salad, and matcha latte
- The visual projections are stunning and very photo-worthy
- Right in Shibuya, so super convenient if you want to continue exploring

This one feels more "up close with the digital art" rather than the animals, but the projection art is incredibly unique to Japan.
